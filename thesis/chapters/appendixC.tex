\chapter{Optimization Derivation: Mean Absolute Deviation} \label{app:mad}

In this appendix, we derive the optimization problems used in the main text for portfolio
optimization under the mean absolute deviation (MAD) risk measure.
We begin by defining the MAD risk risk measure and formulate the base portoflio 
optimization problem. We then introduce regularization techniques and extend the 
formulation to the multi-currency setting, considering sequential overlay optimization 
and joint optimization approaches. Finally, we discuss the implementation of 
the optimization problems in Python using the CVXPY library.
