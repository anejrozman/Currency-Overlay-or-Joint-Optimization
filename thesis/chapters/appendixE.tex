In this appendix, we provide details on the numerical implementation of the optimization problems

ensure efficient and accurate solutions. Additionally, we discuss the 
computational complexity of the optimization problems and provide insights 
into the performance of the implemented algorithms across different scenarios.

\section{The CVXPY Library}\label{sec:cvxpy}

The optimization problems were implemented using the CVXPY library in Python, 
which provides a user-friendly interface for defining and solving convex optimization problems. For the 
considered risk measures in the thesis, we implemented the optimization 
problems as linear programs where possible, quadratic programs in most cases due to the $L_2$ regularization norm, 
and second-order cone programs for the second-order expected shortfall risk measure.
